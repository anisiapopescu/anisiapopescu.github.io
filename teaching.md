# Current Teaching - Université Paris 8 

**Phonétique et Phonologie (undegraduate level)**
This course introduces the study of speech sounds and their organization in human languages. It explores how speech sounds are produced, transmitted and perceived, as well as how they are cognitively represented and systematically organized within linguistic systems. Topics include speech articulation, acoustics, phonetic analysis, and fundamental concepts in phonological theory, such as sound patterns, contrasts and phonological processes. The course also examines the diversity of sound systems across the world’s languages. 

**Phonétique expérimentale et sociophonétique (graduate level)**
The class explores first language acquisition from an experimental phonetics and phonology perspective. It examines how infants and children acquire speech sounds, develop phonological categories, and learn to distinguish words. Topics include speech perception, developmental stages and cross-linguistic variation in language acquisition, as well as the acquisition of two (or more) phonological systems in bilingual children.

**Production et compréhension orale 1,2,3 (undergraduate level)**
The class focuses on the development of English oral production skills, with particular emphasis on pronunciation and prosody. Topics include word stress, rhythm, intonation and connected speech, as well as variation across English dialects and accents.
