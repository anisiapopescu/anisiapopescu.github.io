<h3>Discover lisero – Available on iOS and Android</h3>

<p>Discover <strong>lisero</strong>, a mobile application designed to support learning Romanian Sign Language (LSR). Developed in collaboration with Fundația CODA Farmecul Tăcerii, lisero makes language learning more accessible through an engaging digital experience.</p>
